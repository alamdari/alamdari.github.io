Insights from 2025 for FIFA 2026

Peak times, top sites, and where there is more room across the day.

Based on Niagara Parks Commission data

Key insights

  • Journey Behind the Falls (summer 2025): busiest period is late morning to early afternoon.
  • Lower-traffic hours include roughly 7–8 AM and 8–9 PM.
  • High-traffic days are concentrated in a relatively small share of the period covered.
  • About 62% of scans are concentrated in the three busiest attractions.

Opportunity windows

  • Before 10 AM: clearest low-pressure window.
  • 10 AM – 2 PM: highest pressure; avoid adding concentration.
  • After 5 PM: secondary opportunity window, where operating hours allow.

Planning implications

  • Midday peak (~10 AM – 2 PM) is the primary timing constraint.
  • Early and late hours are the clearest opportunities to redistribute demand.
  • Demand is concentrated in a small number of sites, creating localized pressure.

FIFA context

The FIFA tournament overlaps with Niagara’s peak summer season, creating natural opportunities to plan visits around rest days and days without a Toronto match.

Indicative recommendations

  • Timing: Shift interest toward shoulder hours, especially early morning and evening.
  • Flow: Use hourly and site patterns to stagger entry or experience order during peak periods.
  • Focus: Prioritize coordination at the highest-volume sites, where pressure is most concentrated.
  • FIFA alignment: Use rest days and days without a Toronto match as planning windows.

This summary highlights where coordination can have the most immediate impact. Additional data on attraction capacity, operating constraints, accommodation patterns, and visitor movement between sites would help turn it into operational planning.
